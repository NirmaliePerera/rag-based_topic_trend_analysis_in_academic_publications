# nlp-research-trend-analysis
"An NLP-Based Model for Detecting Research Trends from User-Defined Keywords in Academic Publications" is my final year research project. 

## The Research Problem
The research problem that is beign addressed through this research project is the need for a system to analyse the trend in research topics.
The rapid expansion of the domain of academic research with thousands of papers being published daily across multiple disciplines, presents a challenge for researchers and institutions in identifying emerging trends and shifts in knowledge areas. Traditional manual reviews are time-consuming and often fail to capture timely insights. Hence, there arises a need for automated approaches to detect and present research trends effectively
